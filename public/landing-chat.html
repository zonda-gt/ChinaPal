A real trip · 10 days · Chengdu & Chongqing

This is what your China trip
actually looks like.

01
Where do we eat tonight — somewhere actually local?
CP

Skip the chains. Two streets back — red stools on the pavement, no English menu. Get the 牛油 broth, order duck intestine and lotus root, and say 微辣 unless you really mean it.

02
Where do locals actually drink tea — not a tourist spot?
CP

Old Guanyinge teahouse — bamboo chairs, a few yuan a cup, ear-cleaners working the room. Go mid-morning, sit by the window, stay an hour. No one rushes you.

03
Which train to Guiyang — and where do we sit?
CP

Chongqing to Guiyang — book a left-side window, second class. The line runs through the gorges for an hour. Don't sleep through it.

04
How do we see the pandas without the crowds?
CP

Be at the gate for opening, 7:30, and go left — not right. That's where the cubs are. You'll get them almost to yourself before the tour buses land.

05
We have a spare day — anywhere worth it?
CP

Add a night in Fenghuang on the way back — riverside old town, lit up after dark. Stay on the water, not the new side. You'll thank me.
