A real trip · 10 days · Chengdu & Chongqing

This is what your China trip
actually looks like.

01
Where do we eat tonight — somewhere actually local?
CP

Skip the chains. Red stools two streets back, no English menu. Get the 牛油 broth — and say 微辣 only if you mean it.

02
Where do locals actually drink tea — not a tourist spot?
CP

Old Guanyinge teahouse — bamboo chairs, a few yuan a cup. Go mid-morning, sit by the window, stay an hour.

03
Which train to Guiyang — and where do we sit?
CP

Chongqing to Guiyang — left-side window, second class. An hour through the gorges. Don't sleep through it.

04
How do we see the pandas without the crowds?
CP

Be at the gate for 7:30 opening, then go left — that's the cubs. Yours almost alone before the tour buses.

05
We have a spare day — anywhere worth it?
CP

Add a night in Fenghuang — riverside old town, lit after dark. Stay on the water, not the new side.
