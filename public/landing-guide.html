Before you ask

The honest answers.

Is this a real guide or an AI bot? +
Real people. The $39 Pass is a local team in China answering by text — reserving, translating, recommending, and fixing things in real time. (We also have a free AI planner if you want a quick draft itinerary — but the paid part is human.)
Is it really as good as an in-person guide? +
For the moments that actually matter — what to eat, what to skip, reservations no English line will take, reading a menu, fixing a rained-out day — yes, and for a fraction of the price. What we don't do is walk beside you for eight hours. Most travelers find that's exactly what they didn't want anyway.
What if I want a guide in person for one day? +
Tell us and we'll line up a vetted local guide for that specific day. The Pass covers everything around it — planning, food, translation, and on-the-ground help — for your whole trip.
Do I have to book hotels and tickets through you? +
No, and we'd rather you didn't. Book on Trip.com or wherever's cheapest. We make zero money on your bookings — so unlike a commission-paid guide, we have no reason to ever steer you wrong.
How fast does a real person reply? +
Usually within the hour, and fast when you're mid-problem. It's a considered, in-language, on-the-ground answer — the thing you're actually paying a local for.
